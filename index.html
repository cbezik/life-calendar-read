life calendar read

A personal tool, run by one person on their own computer, that reads their own Google Calendar to find their free time, and puts their own to-dos on a calendar they own as short events with a reminder.

What it does

  • Twice a day, it reads upcoming events from calendars the owner chose, using the read-only Google Calendar permission (calendar.readonly).
  • On one calendar the owner owns, it creates, moves and deletes the events it placed there itself (calendar.events.owned). It never changes or deletes any other event, and asks for no other Google data (no mail, no files, no contacts).
  • It is not offered to anyone else. There is no sign-up and no other user.
